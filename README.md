# Specimen / Lab Requisition Scanner — proof of concept

**Proof of concept. Not for clinical use. Always verify against the paper requisition.**

A small web app (PWA) for community nursing that reads the ticked tests on a photographed BC lab requisition (LifeLabs D2350 or a printed EMR test list) and shows them as a checklist for the nurse to confirm.

Live demo: https://kimberleywilliams00-ai.github.io/specimen-scanner-poc/

## Privacy design
- **Everything runs on the device.** Image processing (OpenCV.js) and text recognition (Tesseract.js) are bundled in this repo and run in the browser.
- **Nothing is stored or sent.** Photos stay in memory only and are cleared on *Finish* / *Discard*. No uploads, analytics, cookies, localStorage or IndexedDB. A strict Content-Security-Policy only allows the app's own files.
- The service worker caches only the app's own static files (for offline use); it never sees or stores photos.
- This repository contains no patient data or requisition photos.

## Updates (v3.1+)
The service worker is network-first for the app itself (page, app JS/CSS, `version.json`), so a new deploy loads on the next open while online; the cache is only an offline fallback. Large engine files under `vendor/` are cache-first (cache `req-scanner-vendor-N`; bump N if they change). The page checks for updates on open and when brought back to the foreground, reloads once when a new version takes over (only when idle), and shows *Update available - tap to reload* if `version.json` differs from the running version. The version is shown on the start screen and in the footer.

To release a new version, bump it in `version.json`, `js/app.js` (`APP_VERSION`), `sw.js` (`VERSION`, `Q`) and the `?v=` query strings in `index.html`.

## Collection Summary (v3.2+)
After **Confirm tests** the app shows a *Collection summary*: what to collect grouped by tube/container (sharing rules: see v3.3 below; centrifuged LifeLabs SST vs unspun SST for BCCDC are always separate), time-critical/fill requirements, do-not-centrifuge warnings, patient preparation (incl. instructions read from the requisition), processing, storage/transport, extra requisition forms and destination, labelling, and the source of every line. Tests without any data get an amber *No collection info yet* card - nothing is guessed. A general CLSI order-of-draw note is labelled as general guidance.

Data: `js/collection_data.js`, transcribed verbatim from LifeLabs Lab Test Information (Lower Mainland) entries exported and verified by the nurse (each entry carries its *Last Published Date* and *last checked* date). It is generated by a build script that checks every value against the source text; the source PDFs are not part of this repository. Directory: https://tests.lifelabs.com/. **Prototype - verify against current LifeLabs/BCCDC guidance before collecting.** The summary lives in memory only; *Done / clear* wipes it.

## Tube rules, local practice and requisition forms (v3.3+)
The Collection summary assigns tubes in this order: **(a)** the LifeLabs *Quick Reference Collection Chart, Lower Mainland and BC Region* (Doc#49141, effective 2024-04-08; Lower Mainland columns) encoded in `js/tube_rules.js` - tube type, size and colour, *Mix N times*, and which tests share **one** tube vs need **their own** tube; tests from different chart groups never share and chart tube sizes override per-test container sizes (differences are flagged); **(b)** sharing rules stated in the per-test LifeLabs Lab Test Information entries (`js/collection_data.js`), plus the combined-tube instruction printed on the BCCDC PHL *Serology Screening Requisition* (PHRQ_HVS_0001F, Version 1.0 August 2026; `js/requisition_forms.js`); **(c)** otherwise a separate tube marked *confirm with lab* (no volume-based sharing). BCCDC unspun serology is never put in a centrifuged LifeLabs tube. Tests that are only on the chart (or only on the PHL form) get a tube card but are marked *processing/storage not yet available*. Where the chart is visually ambiguous, the app shows our reading as *Confirm with lab*.

Local practice rules (`js/local_rules.js`) are kept separate from LifeLabs data and carry their own source and date (VCH community practice, K. Williams, 2026-10-10): **collect a full tube** for every blood tube (per-test volumes are shown only as *lab minimum*), and for **PTT** the summary asks *Client on heparin or unknown anticoagulant?* - *Yes*/*Unsure* shows a red card (cannot be collected in community; redirect to a LifeLabs Patient Service Centre) and PTT is left out of the tubes; the citrate-tube instructions for PTT appear only after *No*. The answer is held in memory only.

Source documents (chart, form, per-test PDFs) are not part of this repository; the data files are checked against their text by build scripts.

## Local practice tube rules (v3.4)
Three further local practice rules (`js/local_rules.js`, source *VCH community practice (K. Williams), 2026-10-11*) take priority over the LifeLabs chart and per-test entries where they conflict:
1. **HCV RNA** (Hep C viral load / PCR; HCV genotyping shares that tube per the BCCDC form) and **HIV Viral Load** each get their **own 6.0 mL EDTA** lavender tube (the chart's *confirm with lab* flag for this is removed).
2. **BCCDC serology tube:** any combination of syphilis, HIV, hepatitis A/B/C serology (incl. Hep A IgM, Hep B core/surface Ab/Ag, HBeAg, Hep B screen/carrier, acute/chronic hepatitis profiles) and other serology goes in **one SST** for BCCDC, following the BCCDC PHL Serology Screening Requisition (PHRQ_HVS_0001F); prenatal panel = 2 SSTs. This replaces the chart's separate Hep A IgM/Hep B tube and two tubes per profile. Per-test LifeLabs centrifuge instructions are kept; when they differ within the tube, one amber note says *LifeLabs pages differ on centrifuging for some of these tests - confirm with BCCDC/LifeLabs* (the tube is not split).
3. Communicable disease tests (HIV, hepatitis, syphilis) go to BCCDC and **never share a tube** with non-communicable tests (lipids, chemistry, etc.) - enforced in the tube planner.

## Serology SST handling and BCCDC eLab Handbook (v3.5)
- **Local practice** (`js/local_rules.js`, *VCH community practice (K. Williams), 2026-10-11*): the BCCDC serology SST is **clotted, then centrifuged** in community. This replaces the v3.4 amber *LifeLabs pages differ on centrifuging* note and the red *Do NOT centrifuge* banner for that tube. Each test's LifeLabs wording stays visible in its test details, labelled *LifeLabs page text*.
- **Clot and centrifuge settings:** when a test in the tube has a BCCDC eLab Handbook entry, the app uses that entry, because the tube goes to BCCDC. Otherwise it uses the LifeLabs SST settings already in `js/collection_data.js` (clot ≥ 30 min, then 10 min at 1300 RCF). Any difference is shown.
- **BCCDC eLab Handbook entries** (`js/elab_data.js`, verbatim; checked against the page text by `tools/check_elab.js`; the source page is not in this repository). So far there is one entry: *Hepatitis C Serology* (Last Changed Thursday, May 22, 2025). Its page lists two specimens:
  - Clotted blood (Preferred), SST (gold top) blood tube, Min 3 mL: allow blood to clot upright at room temperature for about one hour.
  - Serum, Sterile tube, Min 1 mL: centrifuge 10 minutes at 1200 rcf, then transfer the serum fraction to a sterile tube.
  
  Shipping: refrigerate where appropriate. Requisition: PHRQ_HVS_0001F (Version 1.0 August 2026). Each test card shows its own eLab block, labelled with that source, next to the LifeLabs entry. Where the two differ, BCCDC applies for BCCDC-bound tests and the difference is listed. Printed URL: www.elabhandbook.info/phsa. (The v3.5 amber *confirm with BCCDC* serum-transfer note was removed in v3.6.)

## Serology SST: original tube, no serum transfer (v3.6)
**Local practice** (*VCH community practice (K. Williams), 2026-10-11*): after clotting and centrifuging, the BCCDC serology SST is sent **in the original SST; serum is never transferred** to another tube. The tube card says *Clot, then centrifuge. Send in the original SST; do not transfer serum (VCH community practice).* The verbatim eLab text, including its serum-transfer option, stays in the test details, labelled as eLab text.

## HIV and syphilis serology; serology tube card (v3.7)
- **Serology SST tube card:** shows only the eLab **clot** and **centrifuge** sentences (*Allow blood to clot in the tube, standing upright at room temperature for about one hour.* / *Centrifuge the tube for 10 minutes at 1200 rcf.*). It no longer quotes *Transfer the serum fraction to a sterile tube* or any other wording that implies serum transfer, because that contradicts the local rule above. The full verbatim eLab text (both specimen options) is still in each test's details.
- **New BCCDC eLab Handbook entries** (`js/elab_data.js`, verbatim; `tools/check_elab.js` now checks each entry against its own page text):
  - *Human Immunodeficiency Virus 1 and 2 Serology* (Last Changed Friday, May 15, 2026). Turnaround: Screening EIA 1-3 days, confirmation immunoblot 3-5 days.
  - *Syphilis Serology* (Last Changed Wednesday, October 18, 2023). Turnaround: EIA screening 1-3 days, confirmatory testing 3-5 days. Also lists neonate specimens (clotted blood 0.7 mL min; serum 0.35 mL min).
  
  Both entries give the same specimens as Hep C: clotted blood in an SST (Preferred, min 3 mL, about 7 mL drawn, clot about one hour upright at room temperature), or serum (centrifuge 10 min at 1200 rcf). Shipping: refrigerate where appropriate. Requisition: PHRQ_HVS_0001F.
- **New LifeLabs entries** (`js/collection_data.js`, built and verbatim-checked by the pipeline): *HIV SEROLOGY* (published 2025-11-06) and *SYPHILIS SCREEN (BLOOD)* (published 2023-11-29). Both say: SST gold top 5.0 mL, invert 5-6 times, Do NOT centrifuge, refrigerated, TAT 3 days. Syphilis gives a 3 mL collection volume; HIV gives none. HIV adds *If the patient requests non-nominal reporting see: HIV CONFIDENTIAL*, shown under Requisition notes and as a difference (the eLab page has no nominal/non-nominal note).
- **BCCDC first:** these tests go to BCCDC, so the eLab entry takes priority. Each test card lists how the eLab and LifeLabs pages differ (centrifuging, clotting, volume, storage, turnaround, non-nominal note). The local serology tube rules still apply: one BCCDC serology SST, clot then centrifuge, original SST, no transfer, full tube, and never shared with non-communicable tests.
- **Clot/spin settings with several eLab entries in the tube:** the settings from every eLab entry in the tube are compared. When they agree (as Hep C, HIV and syphilis do today), they are shown once with every source named. When they differ, the tube uses the **longest clot** and an amber note lists each entry's settings; if the centrifuge settings differ too, the note says to confirm with BCCDC. Identical eLab patient-prep and frozen-sample notes are shown once for all tests they apply to.
- **Scanner dictionary:** AKA names from the two LifeLabs pages are added through the existing collision rules. Reviewers can also withhold an AKA by hand (`akaWithhold` in the entry): ART, STS, HIV 1, the HIV pooled NAT/NAAT names and *Human Immunodeficiency Virus* are withheld. Short names (HIV AB, RPR, TPPA, VDRL, MHA-TP) are withheld automatically; RPR was already a dictionary synonym.
- **Thyroid (LifeLabs TSH, published 2026-10-09; FREE T4, published 2026-01-20):** both pages say SST gold top 5.0 mL, invert 5-6 times, clot ≥ 30 min, centrifuge 10 min at 1300 RCF, refrigerated, lab minimum 1 mL. TSH stability is 8 days at room temperature, refrigerated or frozen; the FREE T4 page states no stability. The scanner's *TSH (monitor thyroid therapy)* / *TSH* line links to TSH, and a new dictionary test *Free T4* links to FREE T4. *TSH +/- fT4 (suspected hypothyroidism)* (also read as *TSH=/- fTS/T4*) shows **both** entries; one dictionary test can now link to several LifeLabs entries. Both stay in the chart's shared chemistry SST. Withheld AKAs: *Thyroid Function Test*, *Thyroid Studies* (too generic) and the T3-uptake / short names (ETR, FTI, FT4, T3U, T3RU, T3UT, T3 Uptake, T3 Resin Uptake). The hyperthyroid panel (*TSH, fT4 +/- fT3*) is not linked to these entries yet.
- **Electrolytes (LifeLabs pages ELECTROLYTES SERUM (Na & K), published 2023-11-29; ELECTROLYTES SERUM (CL &CO2), published 2025-01-23; ELECTROLYTES EXTENDED PANEL, published 2023-11-29):** The extended-panel page says *Electrolytes or LYTES Extended Panel is a request for NA, K, CL and CO. Due to MSC regulations, panel testing is not available; therefore, unless the medical practitioner specifically requests CLCO in addition to Electrolytes, only the Sodium and Potassium can be entered.* The scanner maps it this way:
  - The *Electrolytes* line goes to the **Na & K** page. That card quotes the extended-panel text, and a note on the card and the tube says chloride/CO2 are **not** assumed to be included.
  - Ticked *Chloride* / *Bicarbonate (CO2)* lines go to the **Cl & CO2** page: *Do NOT expose to air*, *Do NOT pour off*, not possible if hemolysed.
  - When both Na & K and Cl & CO2 are ordered, the note is dropped.
  - All three stay in the chart's shared chemistry SST: clot ≥ 30 min, then 10 min at 1300 RCF, refrigerated, lab minimum 0.5 mL.
  - Withheld AKAs: *SODIUM AND POTASSIUM* and *CHLORIDE AND BICARBONATE* (each names two separate dictionary tests). *LYTES* and *CLCO* are too short. The two page titles are too similar to each other.
  - The extended-panel page is a rule about ordering, not a test, so it has no tube.

## Using it on an iPhone
1. Open the link above in Safari (first load downloads ~25 MB of engine files, then works offline).
2. Optional: Share → *Add to Home Screen*.
3. Tap **Take photo**, photograph the test list, review the results, and check every item against the paper form before confirming.
